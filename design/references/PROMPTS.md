# Visual reference pack: prompts

**No image in this pack has been generated.** The attached image generator
was checked on 2026-11-10: free plan, 0 credits, 0.25 credits per image.
These prompts are ready to run when credits exist, or to paste into any
other generator. Generated images are direction only and never ship
(`docs/08_art_direction.md`, asset classes).

Save each result as `design/references/<Folder>/<filename>.png` and add a
line to that folder's `INDEX.md` with the prompt id and one sentence on what
the image is for.

Rules for every prompt: original designs only; no artist names, no game or
film titles, no existing characters. If a result resembles something that
exists, discard it.

## Style block (append to every prompt)

> Stylised painterly concept art with clean shape design. Palette of warm
> pale vellum stone, blue-black iron-gall ink shadows, verdigris bronze, with
> gold leaf and vermilion used sparingly as accents. Supernatural effects are
> drawn, not glowing: ruled lines, wax seals, tally marks, gilt margins, ink.
> Large areas of calm blank surface, ornament only where authority sits.
> No text, no logos, no watermark.

## Priority set (run these first, in order)

### P1. Master key art  `Art_Direction/key_art_01.png`  16:9
A lone figure in a long asymmetric witness-coat stands on a vast pale stone
floor ruled with faint lines like a page. In front of them a single thin
brass line runs across the floor from edge to edge, perfectly straight, and
everything beyond the line is a monumental courthouse city hanging from
chains that attach to nothing in an overcast gold sky. On the near side of
the line the stone is blank. One vermilion wax seal, the size of a cartwheel,
is pressed into the floor between the figure and the line. Seen from behind
and slightly above, the figure small against the architecture. Quiet, exact,
enormous.

### P2. The protagonist  `Characters/witness_01.png`  3:4
Full-figure character design, neutral stance, front and three-quarter back.
An adult of ambiguous origin in a long travelling coat cut asymmetrically:
one sleeve full, the other arm bound in cord to the elbow. High collar that
reads as a clear shape at distance. Layered cloth, no armour plates, worn
boots. A small lead seal on a cord at the throat, the mark of a sworn
witness. Practical, specific, unglamorous face. Plain pale background, strong
silhouette, flat lighting for design clarity.

### P3. First region: the Assize Quarter, Veyr  `World/veyr_assize_01.png`  16:9
Wide establishing view of a district of law-courts built in pale stone,
composed like pages: broad blank walls with dense carving only around doors
and seals. Verdigris bronze roofs and gutters. Colonnades like ruled lines.
Above the district, an upper city hangs inverted from great chains that
vanish into cloud. In the street, short sentences of written light hang in
the air above people's heads like unpaid promises (shown as abstract line
marks, not legible text). High overcast with pale gold breaks. A market of
oath-sellers under awnings at street level for scale.

### P4a. The Lintel  `Instruments/lintel_forms.png`  16:9
Weapon design sheet, three forms left to right on a plain ground with a
human silhouette for scale. A surveyor's stave as tall as a person, dark
wood with brass rule markings along its length and a folding crossbar at the
head, like half a doorframe. Form one: crossbar folded. Form two: crossbar
open, the stave standing upright on its own. Form three: the crossbar alone,
carried across the shoulders like a yoke. Callouts of the hinge, the planted
foot spike, the brass graduations.

### P4b. The Tally  `Instruments/tally_forms.png`  16:9
Weapon design sheet, three forms. A heavy reliquary gauntlet for the left
hand in dull bronze with a small glazed window on the back, and a cord of
large counting-beads wound up the forearm. Form one: cord wound tight, beads
dull. Form two: cord unwound and trailing, some beads lit from within in
warm gold. Form three: no beads, the bare cord tied around the bearer's own
wrist. Callouts of bead locks, knuckle plate, cord anchor.

### P4c. The Counterpart  `Instruments/counterpart_forms.png`  16:9
Weapon design sheet, three forms. A single-edged blade with a mirror-polished
flat, carried with a second, empty hilt. Form one: one blade and one bare
hilt. Form two: the second hilt now carries a blade that is visibly a
reversed reflection, silvered and slightly translucent. Form three: two
identical blades, neither distinguishable as the original. Callouts of the
polished flat, the paired guards, the scabbard for a blade that is not there.

### P5a. Threshold effects  `Axioms/threshold_board.png`  16:9
Effects board, four panels. A hairline of brass light ruled across a stone
floor between two end-marks. A sword stopped dead in the air exactly at that
line, with a small hinge-shaped flash at the contact. The same line closed
into a perfect ring. A figure stepping through the line where it opens like
a door frame. Hard edges, no glow, no fog.

### P5b. Debt effects  `Axioms/debt_board.png`  16:9
Effects board, four panels. Tally marks scoring themselves into the air
beside a struck fighter. Counting-beads sliding along a cord and locking.
A balance beam tilting above two fighters. A single strike landing with all
the tally marks collapsing into it at once. Wax and bead materials, warm
gold marks on blue-black.

### P5c. Reflection effects  `Axioms/reflection_board.png`  16:9
Effects board, four panels. Two figures joined by a fold line down the
middle of the image like a folded ink blot, the same wound appearing on
both. A sword strike and its exact mirrored copy arriving from the opposite
side of a target. A blow turned back along its own path. A polished blade
showing a scene that is not behind it. Silvering, bilateral symmetry.

### P6. Unveiling  `Transformations/unveiling_lintel.png`  16:9
Sequence board, three frames, same character. The witness plants the stave.
The crossbar unfolds and the stave stands by itself while the brass rule
markings lift off the wood and straighten into a line across the floor. The
witness walks through their own line as through a doorway, coat lifting,
the stone on either side visibly different in tone. The environment reacts:
dust settles into ruled rows.

### P7. Aurel Voss and the Hall of Standing  `Bosses/voss_board.png`  16:9
Boss board. Left: a tall narrow elderly judge in a floor-length stole of
undyed wool with lead seals at the hem, holding upright a long-hafted
seal-press like a staff of office; calm, tired, courteous. Centre: his
silhouette. Right: his arena, a circular hearing hall of pale stone with a
blank floor on which three large vermilion wax seals have been stamped, each
with a faint ruled boundary. Top strip: the moment he brings the seal-press
down and a seal spreads across the floor.

### P8. HUD and Codex  `UI/hud_codex_01.png`  16:9
Interface mock-up over a muted gameplay frame. Bottom left: two thin ruled
lines for health and poise. Right edge: a column of ten small gilt ticks.
Above it one line of small capitals (use placeholder marks, not real
words). At the centre of the fight, a single small pen-stroke glyph with a
short underline. Above the enemy, three stacked short lines, one of them a
solid matte black bar. Opaque vellum panels with a single ink rule. No
glass, no glow, no icons in circles. Second panel: a Codex page laid out
like a manuscript leaf with wide margins and one entry struck through.
